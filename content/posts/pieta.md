***Pietà – Michelangelo’s MasterpiecPietà*** 

Michelangelo’s Pietà, created between 1498 and 1499, is one of the most celebrated sculptures of the Renaissance. Carved from a single block of Carrara marble, it depicts the Virgin Mary tenderly holding the lifeless body of Jesus after his crucifixion. The work captures an extraordinary balance between realism and idealism—Mary’s youthful, serene face contrasts with the tragedy of the moment, symbolizing purity, compassion, and divine acceptance. Michelangelo’s meticulous attention to anatomical detail and the smooth, flowing drapery of Mary’s robes showcase his unmatched skill in sculpting marble.
